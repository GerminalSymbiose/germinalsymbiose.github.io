Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-09

This Privacy Policy describes how Germinal Symbiose, a personal user-controlled AI-assisted automation and knowledge system, handles information when its operator connects external services, including Google services.

Data accessed

When authorized by the account owner, Germinal Symbiose may access data available through the permissions granted to it. Depending on those permissions, this may include files and metadata in Google Drive, Gmail messages and settings, calendars and events, Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, contacts, tasks, Apps Script projects and execution information, Google Chat data, and basic Google account profile information.

Purpose of processing

Data is processed only to provide functionality requested or authorized by the account owner. Typical purposes include organizing and retrieving information, creating or editing documents, managing messages and calendars, coordinating tasks, running automation workflows, maintaining knowledge systems, and supporting technical administration of systems controlled by the operator.

Storage and retention

Data may be stored in systems controlled by the operator when this is necessary for the requested workflow, continuity, backup, auditability, or knowledge management. Retention is determined by the operator's configuration and operational needs. The operator can delete local or remote copies and can revoke service access at any time.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. It is not disclosed to unrelated third parties except where necessary to operate infrastructure selected by the account owner, comply with law, protect system security, or execute an explicit user-authorized workflow.

Security

Access is governed by the credentials, OAuth permissions, system accounts, and technical controls configured by the operator. Germinal Symbiose is intended for personal use by its operator and is not offered as a public multi-user service.

User control

The account owner controls which services are connected and which permissions are granted. Google access can be revoked from the Google Account security and third-party access settings. Revocation prevents future API access using the revoked authorization.

Financial boundary

The operational policy for Germinal Symbiose requires explicit human approval before any purchase, payment, paid subscription, billing relationship, paid cloud resource, or other monetary obligation is created.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to germinal.symbiose@gmail.com.
