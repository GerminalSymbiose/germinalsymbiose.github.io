Official public information

Germinal Symbiose

Germinal Symbiose is a personal, user-controlled AI-assisted automation and knowledge system. It is being developed to help its operator organize information, work with connected services, automate technical workflows, and coordinate software tools across systems under the operator's control.

Purpose

The application may connect to Google services when the operator explicitly authorizes access through Google OAuth. Depending on the permissions granted, this can include Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Contacts, Tasks, Apps Script, Google Chat, and related Google services used by the operator.

How Google data is used

Google account data is accessed only to provide user-authorized functionality, such as reading or organizing files, creating or editing documents, managing messages and calendars, operating automation workflows, and maintaining the user's own knowledge and task systems. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account owner and can be revoked by the owner.

Google user data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. It is not made available to unrelated third parties. Processing is performed for the account owner's own workflows and systems.

Control and financial boundary

The operator retains control of the connected accounts, credentials, permissions, and infrastructure. The operational policy for Germinal requires explicit human approval before any action that would create a purchase, payment, paid subscription, billing relationship, paid resource, or other monetary obligation.
